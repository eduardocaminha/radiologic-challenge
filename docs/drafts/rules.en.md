# Rules

Version 2026-10-30.

## 1. What this is

Radiologic Workstation is a public, permanent showcase of radiology workstations built by anyone: viewer, exam worklist, use of AI, an agent to talk to the worklist, and a reporting screen. The goal is to show new ideas for how reading should work, not to compare products. There is no deadline, no prize in money, products or services, and taking part is free. The organizer is Radiologic, a brand of MEDLABZ CONSULTORIA LTDA, CNPJ 62.761.039/0001-89.

## 2. Who can take part

- People aged 18 or older, from any country, alone or as a team.
- Radiologists, residents, other physicians, developers, students, physicists and the curious.
- A person or team may publish more than one project, as long as they are different projects.

## 3. What can be submitted

You can send a whole workstation or just one part of the workflow, and you can build it with AI: what counts is the reading experience, not who wrote the code. A project is accepted when it meets every item below.

- **A demo people can use.** A web project needs a public address that opens ready to try, with demonstration data already loaded, with no sign-up. A desktop, mobile or extended reality project needs a download or TestFlight link and a video of up to 3 minutes.
- **Open source.** The repository must be public and carry a declared open source license. If you build on an existing project (OHIF, Cornerstone3D, vtk.js, dwv and others), declare the base and respect its license.
- **A complete entry.** Name, workstation parts, tagline, description, what makes it different, platform, technology base, source and license of the demo data, and at least one screenshot.
- **Transparency about AI.** If the project sends images, metadata or text to an external service, this must be declared in the entry. The list of models and agents used to write the code is optional and welcome.
- **No live language model in the public demo.** The public demo does not call an LLM in real time. An agent appears with simulated answers or on video, and the entry says which.

Projects that existed before the challenge may take part.

Not accepted: closed source commercial products, pages that only redirect to another product, copies of someone else's project with no contribution of your own, and illegal or offensive content.

## 4. Patient data

- You may not use, in the demo, the exam worklist, the screenshots, the video or anything shown to an agent, any study from a real patient that does not come from a public dataset with a declared license. That includes your own exams and those of relatives or patients, even if you consider them anonymized.
- A report in a demo is fictional text or comes from a public dataset, and may not describe a real patient.
- Use the challenge data kit or another public dataset, and state the source and license in the entry.
- Head studies are accepted only when the source dataset already distributes them in a way that prevents face reconstruction.
- A submission with identifiable data is removed as soon as it is noticed, without prior notice.

## 5. Not for clinical use

The projects published here are experimental, for education and research. They are not medical devices, are not cleared by ANVISA, the FDA or any other authority, and must not be used for diagnosis, to issue a real report or to guide care. The demo of each project must show this notice visibly. Participants may not claim that the project is fit for diagnostic use.

## 6. Moderation

- Every submission and every screenshot is reviewed before it appears on the site. The expected turnaround is up to 7 days.
- Review checks compliance with these rules. It does not judge quality or taste.
- A rejected submission returns to the author with the reason and may be fixed and resubmitted.
- The organizer may remove at any time a project whose demo stays offline for more than 30 days, that no longer complies with the rules, or that is the subject of a substantiated removal request.

## 7. Voting

- Votes are the only form of ranking. There is no jury.
- To vote you sign in with a Google or GitHub account. Each account gets one vote per project and may vote for as many projects as it likes. A vote can be withdrawn.
- Authors may vote for their own project and may ask colleagues and discussion groups for votes.
- Creating accounts only to vote, using automation, and buying or trading votes are not allowed. The organizer may void votes and block accounts when it finds these patterns, and logs every voided vote.
- The site shows vote counts. It does not show who voted for what.

## 8. Monthly highlights

On the first day of each month, the three projects that received the most votes in the previous month join the highlights page. The month follows Brasília time. In a tie, the project approved earlier ranks first. The same project may be highlighted in more than one month. A highlight is public recognition and carries no prize.

## 9. Rights

- Authors keep ownership of their project and of everything they submit.
- By submitting, the author states that they hold the rights to what was sent and grants Radiologic a free, non-exclusive, worldwide permission to display the project's name, description, screenshots, video and links on the site, on social media and in materials promoting the challenge, always with credit.
- The permission lasts while the project is published. If the author deletes the submission, the material leaves the site. Posts already made on social media and the project's name in highlights of closed months may remain.

## 10. Removal and contact

To request removal of a project, or to report patient data, a license violation or any other problem, write to caminha@radiologic.io. Requests about patient data take priority.

Each author is responsible for the content, code, data and operation of their project. Radiologic does not guarantee the availability, security or fitness of the listed projects and is not liable for damage caused by their use.

## 12. Changes

These rules may change. The version in force is always on this page, with its date. Relevant changes are sent by email to authors with a published project. If the Portuguese, English and Spanish versions differ, the Portuguese version prevails.

## 13. Law and venue
